# My Favorite Anime: Naruto 🍥

Welcome to my repository README! Here are some details about one of my all-time favorite anime series.

## 📌 Overview
* **Title:** Naruto (ナルト)
* **Author:** Masashi Kishimoto
* **Genre:** Action, Adventure, Fantasy

---

## ⭐ Main Characters
1. **Naruto Uzumaki** – The hyperactive ninja whose dream is to become Hokage.
2. **Sasuke Uchiha** – The genius rival from the Uchiha clan.
3. **Sakura Haruno** – A skilled medical-nin from Team 7.
4. **Kakashi Hatake** – The legendary Copy Ninja and Team 7's sensei.

> *"I never go back on my word, because that is my ninja way!"* — **Naruto Uzumaki**

---

## 🎬 Why I Like It
* It has amazing action-packed fight scenes.
* The storyline teaches deep lessons about **friendship**, **perseverance**, and **hard work**.
* The character development from childhood to adulthood is incredible.
